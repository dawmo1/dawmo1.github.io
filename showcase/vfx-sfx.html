ROBLOX PORTFOLIO · VFX + SFX

Effects and sound

Thirteen flipbook effects built for Roblox ParticleEmitters, six layered abilities running in Studio, and thirty-four original sound effects with round-robin variations, mixed onto four SoundGroup buses. All assets are made in-house and ship with full rights. Textures are white so the emitter's Color tints them; VFXPresets.lua, ActionVFX.lua and SFXPresets.lua drop them into a game.
